## Standing rules extracted

1. **Repeated per-slide references belong in a references section at the back.**
   A citation the learner sees on every slide stops being read. Keep a source
   marker on the slide; put the full reference at the end.
2. **Motion is paced to the voice, not to the eye.** In a recorded course an
   animation that finishes before the narrator reaches the item is decoration.
   Stagger intervals should match speech, roughly two to three seconds an item.
3. **Animate what the voice walks through; leave static what the learner reads
   at once.** A list the narrator enumerates should build. A reference block or a
   decision record the learner scans should not.
4. **An interaction must show its result unmistakably.** If clicking changes
   something the learner has to hunt for, the interaction has failed.
5. **The Gate 2 sample is a design proof, not a draft deck.** It shows selected
   layouts and motion styles. Once a layout is accepted, the change belongs in
   the full deck.
