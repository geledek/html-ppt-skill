## Standing rules extracted

1. **One accent for emphasis across a deck.** Numbers, highlights and card
   headings pick the same colour. A second accent used "for variety" reads as a
   second meaning the learner then tries to decode.
2. **Do not restate a point on the slide where it is not the point.** A caveat
   that belongs to another slide dilutes this one.
3. **Status labels that repeat their own group heading are noise.** A timeline
   under "when each part applies" does not need every entry labelled APPLICABLE.
