This is the whole roadmap on one page. The inventory is a gate, not the first of four equal items: you cannot designate an owner for a use you have not listed, and you cannot build a route of appeal for a decision you have not found. MAS puts it at section 3.4 of the draft Guidelines. Once you have the inventory, the next three run in parallel. Designate an owner for each consequential use — a person, not a committee, and not the vendor. Build a route by which someone affected can reach a human with the authority to overturn the outcome. And put AI terms into your procurement, because a third of use cases arrive through suppliers and you remain accountable for those. On the right are the things that can wait. Notice they have no dates. Each one is triggered by a condition, because a date on a slide ages badly and a condition stays true. Certification when a contract or a tender scores it. Tooling when tracking by hand stops working. A dedicated function when ad-hoc ownership stops scaling. Screenshot this page. It is the only slide you need to keep.
