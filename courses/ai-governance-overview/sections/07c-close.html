A list of four good intentions is not a plan. Pick your highest-consequence use and fill in five fields. The use case. The accountable owner, by name. What a person affected can do about it. What evidence says it works here. And the next decision, with a date. Here is one worked through. Automated triage of customer complaints. The owner is the Head of Customer Operations, named, not the vendor. The contest path is none today, because complaints escalated by the tool are never re-read by a person before closure. The evidence is a vendor benchmark only; it has never been tested on our own complaint mix. And the next decision is whether to restrict it to a pilot, by the end of this month. That record took four lines and produced two findings and a deadline. Do it for your top three uses and you have the foundation of your governance programme.
