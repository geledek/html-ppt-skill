## Standing rules extracted from this round

These generalise beyond the slides that prompted them and belong in the skill.

1. **A kicker must not repeat the line beneath it.** If the headline or first
   line already says it, the kicker is noise. Kickers are for orientation the
   slide does not otherwise give.
2. **A list of things gets a list layout**, not boxes and not prose.
3. **Every slide needs a connecting sentence in the script.** The narrator should
   not have to read the slide title aloud to bridge from the previous slide.
4. **One slide, one idea.** If a slide carries three unrelated components, split
   it or cut the weakest. Orphan lines that explain nothing are the symptom.
5. **A category list must not contain the thing it categorises.** "AI governance
   has three parts, one of which is governance" is a broken taxonomy.
6. **Prefer the most recent and most local evidence available.** For a Singapore
   audience, a Singapore financial-services source outranks a 2024 UK survey.
7. **Date-stamp jurisdiction slides consistently.** If one says "as at", they all
   do.
8. **A map must be complete for the claim it makes**, and every highlighted
   region must be labelled.
