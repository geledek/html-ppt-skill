## Standing rules extracted

1. **In a recorded course, motion advances on the learner's input, not on a
   timer.** A timed stagger forces the pace on someone who reads faster or slower
   than the narrator, and it cannot be re-watched in step. Space or the forward
   control should step through a build; the next press moves to the next slide.
2. **Never let the first paint differ from the settled layout.** A scale applied
   after DOMContentLoaded renders one frame at the wrong size, which reads as a
   shake on every slide.
3. **Every slide carries a number, including generated ones.** A missing number
   reads as a missing slide.
4. **Cut the hedge that only protects the author.** "Illustrative
   responsibilities", "teaching synthesis", "these figures do not measure…" are
   written for a reviewer, not a learner. Keep provenance in the research brief
   and the references; keep the slide about the subject.
